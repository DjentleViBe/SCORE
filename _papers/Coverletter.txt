This paper relies on a custom dataset, which I have made publicly available (https://github.com/DjentleViBe/SCORE-SET). 
A separate paper describing this dataset has also been submitted for review a few days ago. 
The core motivation for this paper was that language functions much like music. Its structured by rhythm, shaped by melody, and delivered with emotional nuance. 
Just as music conveys meaning through patterns of sound, language uses pitch, stress, and timing to enhance or alter interpretation. 
If we accept that language is a form of music, then advances in modeling musical perception, structure, and generation can directly 
inform the development of LLMs. This perspective opens new possibilities for capturing prosody, emotion, and 
rhythm in text generation, enabling LLMs to better mirror human communication not only in semantics but in expressiveness and style.
