Dear Editors,
I am pleased to submit my manuscript titled : "SCORE: Tokenization-Aware Transformer for Symbolic Music Generation with Expressions" for consideration in your esteemed journal. 

As a guitarist, I have personally experienced creative blocks and sought new sources of musical inspiration. This motivated me to explore music generation approaches specifically designed to assist in overcoming such blocks and to produce expressive, innovative musical ideas tailored to guitar performance. Despite the growing interest in symbolic music generation, there is little to no work specifically addressing electric guitar music that captures expressive techniques and supports phrase-level creativity.

This gap motivated me to explore a tokenisation scheme through symbolic guitar tablature that captures expressive playing techniques and supports phrase-level context modelling through a sequence-level attention mechanism. This approach enhances rhythmic and structural coherence in generated musical sequences while enabling efficient batching of variable-length phrases, tailored to run on my personal desktop. I have mainly used quantitative methods to analyse results, but subjectively I am quite pleased with the phrases that the model is able to produce. 

This work has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. It is not based on any prior conference publication. All relevant permissions and copyrights have been secured. The total word count of this submission, including all notes and references, is 1887. 

I kindly request a standard double-blind review process, with no special considerations required. I believe this contribution aligns with TISMIR’s focus on advancing computational approaches to music information retrieval and generation. Thank you so much for your time and consideration.
